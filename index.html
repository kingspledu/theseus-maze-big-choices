A Greek myth retold

Theseus and the Maze of Big Choices

A story about courage, friendship, promises, and the choices that shape a hero.

Ages 8+ · Target Lexile 210L

Read the complete original text · Character Bible

A Boy in Greece

Long ago, in Greece, there was a boy named Theseus. He lived with his mom, Aethra.

The Big Rock

One day, his mom took him to a big rock. His dad had left a gift under it.

One More Try

Theseus pushed the rock. He pushed again, and at last the rock moved!

A Gift from His Father

Under the rock were a sword and a pair of shoes. They had belonged to his dad, King Aegeus of Athens.

Ready to Go

Aethra told Theseus he was ready to meet his dad. Theseus wanted to make his mom proud.

The Long Road

Theseus could take a boat to Athens. But he chose the long road, even though it was not safe.

Courage on the Road

Bad men hurt people on the road. Theseus was scared, but he fought them and stopped them.

A Safer Journey

Theseus helped keep the road safe. At last, he came to Athens.

Home at Last

King Aegeus saw the sword and shoes and knew his son. They gave each other a big hug.

The Sad Children

Theseus saw a group of sad kids. They had to go to Crete, where the Minotaur lived in a huge maze.

A Brave Choice

The young people sent to the maze did not come home. Theseus felt scared, but he chose to go and help.

Ariadne’s Gift

Princess Ariadne was kind and smart. She gave Theseus a ball of red thread to help him find his way out.

Into the Maze

Theseus let the thread fall as he walked. The dark path went left, then right, then left again.

The Minotaur

Suddenly, the Minotaur was there! Theseus took out his sword and stood tall, even though his hands shook.

The Way Out

Theseus won the fight and followed the thread back. He led the kids out of the maze, and they were free.

Sailing Away

Theseus and Ariadne sailed away together. They came to an island called Naxos.

A Friend Left Behind

Theseus left Ariadne on the island. She had helped save his life, but now she was alone.

The Forgotten Sail

Theseus had promised to put up a white sail if he came home safe. But he forgot, and the ship still had its black sail.

A Father’s Grief

King Aegeus saw the black sail and thought his son was dead. In his grief, he jumped into the sea and never came back.

A Promise Lost

When Theseus got home, he heard the news and cried. He had won a big fight, but he had lost his dad.

One Big Team

Theseus became king and helped bring the towns near Athens together. People could do more when they worked as a team.

A Very Bad Plan

Theseus and Pirithous tried to take Persephone away without asking her. Hades caught them in the land of the dead, and they were stuck.

Help from Heracles

Later, Heracles came and saved Theseus. Being brave had not made his bad plan right.

The Lie

Phaedra told a terrible lie about Hippolytus. Theseus became angry and did not stop to hear his son’s side.

Too Late to Listen

Theseus asked Poseidon to punish his son, and Hippolytus died. Later, Theseus learned that his son had done nothing wrong.

The Last Journey

As an old man, Theseus left Athens and went to Skyros. There, King Lycomedes pushed him off a cliff, and Theseus died.

What Makes a Hero?

Many years later, people brought his bones to Athens and honored him. His story reminds us that a real hero is kind, fair, and true.

The End · A real hero is kind, fair, and true.
